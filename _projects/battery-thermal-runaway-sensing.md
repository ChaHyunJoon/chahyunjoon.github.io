## Overview

This project focused on clarifying the mechanism of EV battery thermal runaway and connecting that understanding to sensing and actuation strategies in underground parking environments.

## Project Details

- Original Title: 배터리 열폭주를 막는 센서 알고리즘 개발
- Period: September 2024 - December 2024
- Affiliation: Korea University

## Summary

The project aimed to identify the principles behind thermal runaway in electric vehicle batteries and connect them to gas-sensor-based detection and downstream actuation in underground parking lots. The broader goal was to support earlier intervention and safer response in confined environments.
